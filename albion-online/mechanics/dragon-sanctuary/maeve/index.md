# Maeve, the Unfettered Oracle

| Stage | Health  | Mechanics                                                                            |
| ----- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1     | 100–80% | Three boulders, Boulder Charge (dash), Boulder Stomp (sparkles/darts) and Realm Rift |
| 2     | 80–60%  | Rock Spikes (red arrow on target)                                                    |
| 3     | 60–40%  | Storm Pulse, Storm Spires (shards), Storm Coil                                       |
| 4     | 40–0%   | Three additional boulders, second Rock Spike target, Primal Dragon Flame             |
{: .nowrap-1 .nowrap-2 }

Rock Spike starts at **80% health or 120 seconds**, whichever comes first. A second Rock Spike target is added at **40% or 240 seconds**, if at least six targets are on Maeve’s threat list. Primal Dragon Flame starts at **40% or 300 seconds**.

## Maeve’s abilities

- Realm Rift
- Rock Spike
- Primal Dragon Flame
- Maeve’s Boulders
	- Boulder Charge
	- Boulder Stomp
- Storm Spires
	- Storm Pulse
	- Storm Coil

### Realm Rift

![Realm Rift](assets/realm-rift.png)

Maeve targets a roughly **4-metre-radius circle** at her current target’s position, with a **1.25-second cast**. The hit deals physical damage and applies a stacking effect that increases damage from subsequent Realm Rifts.

The debuff lasts **100 seconds** and can reach **15 stacks**. Its damage increase applies to Realm Rift; it is not a general increase to all incoming damage. Repeatedly absorbing this attack can turn an initially survivable mistake into a lethal one.

### Boulder Charge

![Boulder Charge](assets/boulder-charge.png)

A boulder prepares for roughly **1.6 seconds**, then charges along its path. The moving boulder deals physical damage and knocks players back; its landing area can also damage and displace nearby players.

Boulders can select players across the arena, with a preference for a distant target when one is available. They can also charge back toward positions around Maeve when the encounter repositions them. Do not assume a returning boulder is harmless.

### Boulder Stomp

![Boulder Stomp](assets/boulder-stomp.png)

A boulder stomps after a short wind-up of about **0.8 seconds**. The initial impact heavily damages players within roughly **3.5 metres**, and the stomp sends **three narrow projectiles** (sparkles/darts) outward in different directions. Maeve commands repeated stomps, producing successive waves of these darts.

The outward projectiles travel roughly **32 metres**. A damaging projectile hit applies a **75-second stacking vulnerability**: later projectile hits increase by **5% of their base damage per stack**, up to **10 stacks**. Several avoidable hits can therefore make the later waves much harder to survive.

### Storm Pulse

![Storm Pulse](assets/storm-pulse.png)

A large circle marks a Storm Spire’s arrival. After roughly **3 seconds of warning**, an expanding ring sweeps outward over about **1.3 seconds**, dealing magic damage and interrupting casting on a hit. The ground warning is about **15 metres in radius**.

The Storm Spire appears as part of this sequence and is briefly protected while spawning. The initial expanding pulse is distinct from the spire’s later **Storm Coil**.

### Storm Coil

![Storm Coil](assets/storm-coil.png)

A surviving Storm Spire casts Storm Coil over **8 seconds**, then creates a damaging lightning ring for about **5 seconds**. The ring deals magic damage every **0.4 seconds** between roughly **5.5 and 16 metres** from the spire.

The immediate inner area, inside about **5.5 metres**, is clear of Storm Coil. Beyond the outer edge is also outside this particular attack. The dangerous part is the band between those two boundaries, where repeated ticks punish remaining in place or crossing slowly.

### Rock Spike

![Rock Spike](assets/rock-spike.png)

Maeve marks a player with a **red arrow**. The first spike placement appears beneath that player **1.5 seconds after marking**, followed by another every **1.1 seconds**, for **12 placements** beneath their successive positions. The final placement appears approximately **13.6 seconds after marking**.

Each placement produces a square warning about **2.75 metres across**, lasting **2 seconds**. The square becomes dangerous approximately **1.5 seconds after appearing** and remains dangerous for roughly **1.5 seconds**, overlapping the end of the visible warning. The final square’s damage area expires approximately **16.6 seconds after marking**. A hit deals damage and can cause a short knockback. The spikes can hit other players as well as the marked player.

Maeve excludes her highest-threat target when choosing the mark. Later in the fight, the additional target slot allows a second player to be marked while the first trail is still being placed.

### Primal Dragon Flame

![Primal Dragon Flame](assets/primal-dragon-flame.png)

Maeve prepares Primal Dragon Flame with a **5-second cast**, followed by an 8-second channel containing **three pulses**. The first pulse is scheduled **2 seconds after the channel begins**, with the next two **3 seconds apart**. The damage effect has a small additional delay, so use the cast and visible hits to adjust execution. There are **six seconds between the first and third pulses**, but roughly **thirteen seconds from the start of the initial cast to the final pulse**. Each pulse combines normal magic damage with an additional **armour-ignoring damage component**.
