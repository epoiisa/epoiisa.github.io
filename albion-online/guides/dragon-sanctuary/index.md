## Clear

- **Support Tank.** Use Threatening Smash (Q), Taunt (R) and Authority (passive) to help the Main Tank keep threat off party other members.

### Mammoth Hunter

- **Ironroot Staffs.** Use Motivating Cleanse (W) on players stunned by the Thundering Javelin (3 second stun).
- **Enigmatic Staffs.** Use Motivating Cleanse (W) on players stunned by the Thundering Javelin (3 second stun) and use Protective Beam (E) on players targeted by the follow-up Mark of the Hunt/Forrest of Spears.

## Maeve

- **Main Tank.** Stay moving in a tight circle around Maeve and prioritise dodging Realm Rift to avoid accumulating stacks. Use Acid Potions on Maeve throughout the fight when party DPS is focused on Maeve.
- **Support Tank.** Use Defensive Slam (Q) occasionally on Maeve to shield the Main Tank. Note that Shrinking Curse (E) has no effect on Maeve or Maeve’s Boulders, and Guardian Armor has no effect on Maeve’s Boulders. At 40%, save Guardian Armor for Primal Dragon Flame.
- **Party Healers.** Use all abilities liberally on the party until 40%, then save Salvation (E) for Primal Dragon Flame.
- **Enigmatic Staffs.** Mimic (W) either tank’s Guard Rune and use all abilities liberally on the party. At 60%, reserve Judicator Armor and Protective Beam (E) for the party taking down Storm Spires (shards). At 40%, save Judicator Armor and Protective Beam (E) for Primal Dragon Flame.

### Maeve’s Boulders

- **Everyone.** Individually dodge Boulder Charges (dashes) by staying mobile and using Evasive Jump (F) only when absolutely necessary.
- **Everyone.** Individually reposition carefully for Boulder Stomp (sparkles/darts). If you are far enough away, there is enough time to walk out of the path of the darts. A few hits won’t hurt you. Repeated hits will. Keep dodging the darts to avoid building stacks, even if you are with the group getting “defensives”.

### Rock Spikes

- Players marked with the **red arrow**, move immediately to the edge of the room and walk slowly in a tight square so you don’t spread Rock Spikes around the room in the way of others. Use Dragonslayer Jacket for 6 seconds of grace (if available).

### Storm Spires (shards)

- **Everyone.** Stay outside the Storm Pulse circle then move quickly to the Storm Spire when safe and destroy it ASAP. During Storm Coil (lightning ring), inside the inner circle is safe, but it is also possible to stand in the lightning if an Enigmatic Staff defends the party with Judicator Armor and Protective Beam (E).
- **Support Tank.** Use Shrinking Curse (E) and Acid Potions on the Storm Spire as soon as party DPS is focused on it.
- **Enigmatic Staffs.** Reserve Judicator Armor and Protective Beam (E) for the party taking down the Storm Spire.
- **Shadowcaller and Blazing Staff.** Coordinate with the **Support Tank** to drop Inner Shadow (E) and Flame Tornado (E) on the Storm Spire. When Maeve is less than 20%, listen to the caller as priority may shift back to Maeve.

### Primal Dragon Flame

- Maeve prepares Primal Dragon Flame with a 5-second cast, followed by an 8-second channel containing three damage pulses. The pulses are scheduled at 2 seconds, 5 seconds, and 8 seconds into the channel.
- During the cast, the call is 🗣️ *Fire! Fire! Fire! Stack on the crown!*
- **Support Tank.** Activate Enfeeble Aura (R) __before__ the cast finishes.
- **Enigmatic Staff 1.** Activate Force Shield (R) and cast Protective Beam (E) immediately __after__ the cast finishes. Protective Beam will last for the first and second damage pulses.
- **Party Healers.** The three damage pulses will be defended primarily by **Party Healers 1, 2 & 3** casting Fallen Staff Salvation (E) in succession. **Party Healers 1, 2 & 3** must call out if they are unavailable so **Party Healer 4** can stand in. The first Fallen Staff must be cast 3 seconds into Maeve’s initial 5-second cast. The second and third Fallen Staffs must be cast in the second before the first and second damage pulses, 1 and 4 seconds into the channel respectively.
- **Enigmatic Staff 2.** Activate Force Shield (R) and cast Protective Beam (E) immediately __after__ the first damage pulse. Protective Beam will last for the second and third damage pulses.
- **Enigmatic Staffs.** Alternate using Lifeward Potion before the last damage pulse (end of the channel) for each round of Primal Dragon Flame.
- **Everyone.** Use Dragonslayer Jacket to cover the first and second or the second and third damage pulses.
- **Everyone.** Use Soldier Helmet and Gigantify Potions to cover the second and third damage pulses.

### Flame summons and Fire Essences

- **Enigmatic Staffs.** You are designated Fire Essence collectors. When they appear, collect three Fire Essences / three Collected Fire Essence stacks and your Q spell changes to Unstable Fire Essence. Go to the Flame summons (spikey balls) and cast Unstable Fire Essence (Q). Alert the group to stand clear before casting Unstable Fire Essence. Note that Collected Fire Essence stacks and Unstable Fire Essence remain for only 8 seconds.

### Giant Rupture

- The call is 🗣️ *In → Out → In.*
- If you are outside the outer circle, stay there.

### Inferno

- Hide behind the rock pillars.

### Meltdown

- Meltdown has a 5-second warning followed by 17 damage pulses, one every 0.5 seconds. The last damage pulse occurs 8 seconds after the first.
- The call is 🗣️ *Meltdown! Meltdown! Meltdown! Stack on the crown!*
- **Support Tank.** Use Forceful Swing (W), Fatal Blade (E), and Guardian Armor __before__ the end of the 5-second cast.
- **Fallen Staffs 1 & 2.** Cast Salvation (E) when the damage starts.
- **Fallen Staffs 3 & 4.** Cast Salvation (E) as the first Fallen Staff Salvation ends.
- **Enigmatic Staffs.** Use Judicator Armor when the damage starts. Just before halfway through the 8-second damage window, use a Lifeward Potion then cast Protective Beam (E).
- **Everyone.** If you have Soldier Helmet and Gigantify Potions, use them halfway through the 8-second damage window.
